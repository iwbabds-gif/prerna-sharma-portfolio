Part · 01 · B2B SaaS

HRTech product for fresh graduate hiring from colleges.

Product: Foster

2 years  |  0–1 Scale  |  B2B SaaS

10,000+ colleges onboarded 6,000+ cold calls 1,500+ campus drives 1,200+ graduates placed 50+ product demo calls 80+ workshops/competitions

Results: 0 → $100K (₹80L+) revenue.
Ownership: Day-to-day operations, end-to-end customer project delivery.

Why it didn't scale

  • Slow adoption due to product rigidity — Mandatory college onboarding before a student could sign up caused a long institutional approval cycle.
  • Supply scaled while demand did not — Supply-side operations scaled into a system. Revenue and the demand side did not, due to resource constraints.
  • The shutdown — After two years, GenAI shifted the org's focus. The product was bleeding money and was closed.

What I learned

  • Narrative matters — We switched from selling a placement management tool to a college discovery tool for employers. This increased adoption on the college side.
  • Effort scales linearly. Systems scale exponentially — Ground efforts don't scale if not built into systems. Input and output stay close together, with no compounding.
  • Resourced to exist ≠ resourced to win — Enough to keep going is not enough to break through. Resource constraints can kill a product faster than the market.
Impact

A complete product autopsy. Watching a 0→1 born, plateau, and shut down end to end has brought me some business sense and a little wisdom.

Part · 02 · Technology Services Sales

B2B technology services GTM.

Company: smartSense

1 year  |  1–10 Scale  |  Enterprise Sales

$1M+ revenue across 4 deals 100+ sales enablement assets

What went wrong / limitations

  • An enablement layer, not an engine — Sales was founder-led. Support work sharpens deals but does not compound into a repeatable demand system.

What I learned

  • How deals actually move — A year inside proposals maps where deals stall and which collateral moves them, and the deal dynamics.
Impact

It sent me downstream for good, and helped me understand the importance of systems for scale.

Part · 03 · Consumer IoT Brand

Connected home ecosystem with centralised control.

Product: DOO

1 year  |  B2C  |  0–1 Scale

5M+ reach 20,000+ high-intent leads 2,000+ inbound leads 20X brand engagement 40X website visits

Results: ₹25L → ₹2Cr (~$30K → $240K) ARR.
Ownership: Monthly/quarterly Ads strategy & budget management, end-to-end brand management.

Limitations

  • Brand lagged behind the funnel — Growth had already outrun the branding.
  • Paid stopped absorbing — Spend kept climbing, the conversion didn't. Sales capacity and process hadn't been built to match the top of funnel.

What I learned

  • Things grow in parallel, or they don't — Funnel, brand, and ops must scale together. One lags, nothing scales.
  • Before scaling the funnel, fix the leaks — Leads outran capacity. Branding inconsistency and thin resources leaked lead-to-demo and demo-to-conversion.
Impact

The three parts became one education — model-level growth reading from Part 1, full-funnel thinking from Part 2. Part 3 is where both met a real revenue number and held. Full-chain ownership, reach to close.
